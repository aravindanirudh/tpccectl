About TPC

A Professional Body Dedicated to Student Success

The Training and Placement Cell is a professional body of the institute coordinated by student representatives under the able guidance of the Principal and faculty. Our main aim is to provide opportunities for technical as well as soft skill development to students in order to improve their employability.

TPC provides all assistance to students to achieve their career goals - from intensive aptitude training to live industry interactions and placement drives with leading companies.

Training Programs
Aptitude Training Quantitative Aptitude Logical Reasoning Technical Aptitude Weekly Aptitude Tests Resume Preparation Interview Dynamics Mock Interviews Essential Geometry
Training and Placement Cell aims to equip the students with the necessary skillset by giving training in emerging areas and also helps the students to kick start their career by providing ample placement opportunities. Our excellent placement record is a fine testament to the corporate outreach that we could establish over the years.
